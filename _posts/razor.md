About three years ago I got a Merkur 34C, but unfortunately the thread on the lid stripped recently, so I'd been having to manually press the lid down while shaving, which is difficult to do quickly.
I thought I'd be using that razor indefinitely, and I quite liked how it held,
but it turns out that the shallow threading wears out quite quickly because the razor is *not* made of stainless steel.
A replacement lid is fairly cheap but fundamentally the parts of a razor which
are in frequent contact with water need to be made of something stronger.
Merkur and Feather have stainless steel razors-- including basically a stainless steel 34C-- but they're over $100.

After some internet searching, I saw that Razorock (a Canadian CNC company?) makes a stainless 34C clone for $60 + $10 shipping.
I'll have to see if everything is still looking good in three years, but it feels okay so far, the handle is just a little narrower and longer.
